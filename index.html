Chrome extension

BETTER AutoLaTeX

Render Google Docs as LaTeX PDFs, preserve rich text and tables, and keep your workflow inside the document.

Download extension (.zip) Installation guide

Get started

Install BETTER AutoLaTeX in Chrome

Follow these three steps once. Afterwards, BETTER AutoLaTeX is available from the menu bar in every Google Doc you open in Chrome.

1

Download and extract the extension

Click Download extension (.zip) above. In your Downloads folder, right-click better-autolatex.zip, click Extract All..., and finish extracting it somewhere you plan to keep it.

2

Load the extension in Chrome

Open chrome://extensions in Chrome. Turn on the Developer mode switch in the upper-right corner. Click Load unpacked, choose the extracted better-autolatex folder, then click Select Folder.

3

Approve the Google Docs formatter

Click the approval button below while signed into the Google account you use for Docs. If Google shows the warning screen, click Advanced, then click Go to Untitled project (unsafe). On the next screen, click Review permissions and complete the Google permission prompt.

Why Google calls it unsafe: this Apps Script has not completed Google's public verification process. That warning does not mean your computer found malware. BETTER AutoLaTeX uses the script only as a formatter for Google Docs; it is not an installer or separate executable. As with any permission screen, continue only when you trust the developer/account shown and intend to use this project.

Use it

Render from Google Docs

Open the menu

Refresh any open Google Doc, then click Better AutoLaTeX in the Docs menu bar.

Choose a destination

Click Render to (Split Screen) to keep the PDF beside your document, or switch it to New Tab.

Render or download

Click Render PDF to preview it, or Download PDF to save it. You can also use Ctrl + Shift + Z and Ctrl + Shift + X.
